The Volt platform

Your entire training life, in one place.

Most athletes train in more than one discipline. Volt is built for exactly that — a single platform where your lifts and your miles live together, inform each other, and add up to something.

Strength, tracked precisely

Every set, rep, and kilogram. Live PR detection, auto-fill from last session, rest timers that start themselves.

Cardio, recorded in full

GPS routes, live pace, heart rate, elevation, segment splits. Your runs sit in the same feed as your workouts.

Analytics that cross disciplines

See how a big cardio week affects your squat. Patterns you can only spot when both data streams are unified.
